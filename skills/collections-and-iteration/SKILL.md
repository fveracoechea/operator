# Collections and iteration

This skill is about the shape of a collection and the walk over it.

The examples assume an ES2023 target, where the immutable array methods exist and no example needs a copy first.

## Pick the task

- **Sorting an array you were given, or dropping the misses of a map that can miss**: [array-methods.md](array-methods.md).
- **Finding items of one collection inside a loop over another**: [lookups.md](lookups.md).
- **Writing a loop with branches or an early exit, or walking the keys of an object**: [loops.md](loops.md).
