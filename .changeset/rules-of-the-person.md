The `operator` skill router now states the five rules of the person, R1 to R5, as a whole, before every topic: the Operator is read-only, nothing merges a pull request without the explicit approval of the person, nothing tears down unlanded work, an Operative never addresses the person directly, and the Operator keeps its own context as low as possible. When a topic or a report seems to ask for something that a rule forbids, the Operator obeys the rule and brings the conflict to the user.
